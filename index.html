Space Battle – Privacy Policy

Developer: RetroArcade · Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Space Battle has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is saved on your phone. If you use Google Play Games, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your Google account and puts your best score on a public leaderboard. The game shows optional rewarded video ads, and an occasional short full-screen ad between runs, both from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: scores, credits, upgrades, ships you own, missions, daily challenge progress, settings (volume, touch speed, visual effects) and whether you own PREMIUM. We do not send this information to any server of ours, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes the copy on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google account (see section 2).

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you have a Google Play Games profile, the game signs in to it when it starts, the standard way Play Games works; you can also sign in from Settings → GOOGLE PLAY in the game. You can play without signing in. When you are signed in:

  • Cloud save: the same progress listed in section 1 is saved to the Saved Games area of your Google account, so it comes back if you reinstall or change phones. It is stored by Google, not by us.
  • Leaderboard: the score of each normal run is sent to the game's global leaderboard. Other players can see your Play Games name, avatar and score there, according to your Play Games visibility settings.
  • Google Play Games itself identifies your profile to the game and may collect device and usage information to run these services.

This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile, its saved games and its leaderboard scores in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a continue or extra credits). It also shows a short full-screen ad when you leave the game-over screen, about every third run, and never while you are playing. Ads are never shown during a run, and the game does not show banner ads. All ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show, measure and prevent fraud in these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

This data is processed by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive your advertising ID or personal information from these ads, and we do not sell your data.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you play, a consent form from Google asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → AD CHOICES in the game.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Fewer ads: rewarded ads are always your choice. Buying PREMIUM removes the full-screen ads between runs and gives every ad reward without watching the ad.

5. Purchases

The optional PREMIUM upgrade (one payment, no subscription) is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. Google shares with us only what is needed to confirm the purchase (such as an order identifier). See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children

Space Battle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and how to delete your data

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. To delete what Space Battle (by RetroArcade) stores about you:

  1. Progress on your phone: uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Space Battle → Storage → Clear data. This deletes scores, SCRAP, upgrades, ships, paints and settings for good.
  2. Google Play Games cloud save and leaderboard scores (only if you signed in): open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile, then Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and delete the data for Space Battle (or your whole profile). You can also manage this at myaccount.google.com. Google deletes it under its own policy.
  3. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads). Google handles ad data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  4. Anything else, or if you need help: email retroarcadegames874@gmail.com with the subject "Space Battle data deletion". We reply within 30 days.

Nothing is kept after these steps, except what Google must keep under its own policies (for example records of purchases).

8. Security

Your saved progress stays on your device, and the optional Play Games cloud copy is kept by Google. Everything the game sends to Google (ads, Play Games, purchases) goes over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
