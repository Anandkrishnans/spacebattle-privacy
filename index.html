Space Battle – Privacy Policy

Developer: RetroArcade · Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: Space Battle has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: scores, credits, upgrades, ships you own, missions, daily challenge progress, settings (volume, touch speed, visual effects) and whether you own PREMIUM. We do not send this information to any server, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a continue or extra credits). Ads are never shown without you asking for one. They are provided by Google AdMob.

To show, measure and prevent fraud in these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

This data is processed by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive your advertising ID or personal information from these ads, and we do not sell your data.

3. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you play, a consent form from Google asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → AD CHOICES in the game.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • No ads at all: you never have to watch an ad to play. Buying PREMIUM gives every ad reward without watching the ad.

4. Purchases

The optional PREMIUM upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. Google shares with us only what is needed to confirm the purchase (such as an order identifier). See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

Space Battle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. For data held by Google in connection with ads, see Google's policy above; you can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

7. Security

Your saved progress stays on your device. Information collected by the ads SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
